# JRM Top Build Ltd — Website Plan

Prepared for: JRM Top Build Ltd (NZBN 9429051799410) · Owner: Ernesto Jr Villa Robarios
Proposed domain: www.jrmtopbuild.nz

## 1. Project snapshot

JRM Top Build is a Christchurch-based commercial and residential building company run out of Hornby, offering renovations and alterations, pergolas, decking, floor planks, and bathroom renovations. The existing logo — a circular silver-and-gold badge with the tagline "Premium · Crafted · Reliable" — already carries a strong, established visual identity (dark navy, brushed silver, warm gold) and a matching set of five thin gold line icons for each service. The website plan below is built to extend that identity rather than invent a new one, so the site feels like the same business the logo represents.

The goal of the site is simple: turn local search traffic and word-of-mouth referrals into quote requests. Most visitors will be homeowners or property/facility managers in Christchurch and Canterbury who found JRM Top Build through Google, a truck sign, or a recommendation, and who want to quickly confirm three things — that JRM does the specific job they need, that the work looks good in photos, and that it's easy to get a quote.

## 2. Goals & audience

Primary goal: generate quote/enquiry form submissions and phone calls.
Secondary goals: rank locally for "builder," "decking," "pergola," "bathroom renovation," etc. in Christchurch/Canterbury; build trust through photos and reviews; make it effortless to check on a phone at a job site or in a truck.

Audience: homeowners planning a renovation, deck, pergola, or bathroom upgrade; small commercial property owners/managers needing alteration work; people comparing 2–3 local builders before requesting quotes.

## 3. Sitemap

A multi-page structure gives each service its own indexable page (better for local SEO than one long homepage) while keeping the total page count manageable for a small business site.

- Home
- About
- Services (hub page)
  - Renovations & Alterations
  - Pergolas
  - Decking
  - Floor Planks
  - Bathroom Renovations
- Gallery
- Testimonials
- Service Area
- Contact / Get a Quote
- Utility: 404 page, Privacy notice (footer link only)

Primary navigation: Home · About · Services (dropdown to the five) · Gallery · Testimonials · Contact. "Get a Quote" stays out of the dropdown clutter and appears as a standalone button in the header on every page.

## 4. Page-by-page content plan

**Home** — Hero with the round logo, headline, tagline, and a "Get a Quote" + "Call now" button pair over a job-site photo. Below the fold: a five-icon services strip (using the existing icon set) that links to each service page; a short "why JRM" trust section (licensed/insured, X years' experience, commercial + residential, Christchurch-owned); a rotating strip of 3–4 gallery photos linking to the full Gallery; 2–3 testimonial quotes; a service-area summary line; and a closing quote-form call to action.

**About** — Owner-led story (Ernesto Jr Villa Robarios / JRM Top Build), what "commercial and residential jobs" means in practice, values behind "Premium · Crafted · Reliable," and any trade qualifications, insurance, or Master Builder–style affiliations worth stating. A face-and-tools photo here builds more trust than stock imagery.

**Services hub** — One paragraph per service (Renovation & Alteration, Pergolas, Decking, Floor Planks, Bathroom Renovation) each paired with its icon and a "Learn more" link into the dedicated page below.

**Five individual service pages** (Renovations & Alterations, Pergolas, Decking, Floor Planks, Bathroom Renovations) — each follows the same template: what's included, a 3–6 photo mini-gallery of past work in that category, typical process/timeline, an FAQ (2–4 questions, e.g. "Do I need consent for a pergola in Christchurch?"), and a quote-form CTA pre-filled with that service type. Separate pages exist mainly so each can rank for its own search term ("deck builder Christchurch," "pergola Christchurch," etc.).

**Gallery** — Grid of completed jobs, filterable by service category (reusing the same five categories/icons), each photo optionally paired with a one-line project caption (suburb + job type, e.g. "Bathroom renovation — Riccarton").

**Testimonials** — Full list of customer quotes/star ratings, plus an embedded or linked Google Business Profile review badge so new reviews show without a rebuild.

**Service Area** — A simple list (not necessarily an interactive map) of Christchurch suburbs and nearby Canterbury/Selwyn areas covered from the Hornby base — e.g. Hornby, Sockburn, Islington, Hei Hei, Riccarton, Addington, Sydenham, Halswell, Hillmorton, Templeton, Rolleston — with a line inviting enquiries from outside the list.

**Contact / Get a Quote** — The conversion page: quote form (see §6), click-to-call mobile number, email, business address, NZBN, and business hours. A small embedded Google Map pin for the Hornby base reassures visitors this is a real, local operator.

**404 / Privacy** — Standard lightweight utility pages; not a priority for launch content but should exist so the site doesn't dead-end or fall foul of basic NZ privacy-notice expectations if the quote form collects personal data.

## 5. Brand & design direction

The existing logo artwork is the starting point for the whole visual system, so the site should look like it was designed by the same hand that designed the badge — dark, metallic, and precise, not a generic "bright blue construction template."

**Logo usage.** The round badge (extracted from the supplied artwork as `logo-720.png` / `logo-160.png`) sits on dark navy in the header, footer, and hero exactly as designed — it was never meant to sit on white, since its silver ring and gold linework are tuned for a dark background. Where a compact mark is needed (favicon, mobile nav), use a tightly cropped version of the same badge rather than redrawing it.

**Colour palette**, sampled directly from the logo:

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary background (dark) | Deep navy | `#0A1017` |
| Secondary panel (dark) | Navy-charcoal | `#121B26` |
| Accent / CTAs | Metallic gold | `#C6A15B` |
| Accent hover / highlight | Bright gold | `#E8C97A` |
| Secondary accent | Brushed silver | `#C7CBCE` |
| Light background (content sections) | Off-white | `#F5F6F7` |
| Body text on light | Near-black ink | `#1B222B` |
| Muted / secondary text | Cool grey | `#8A93A0` |

Pattern: alternate full-bleed dark navy sections (hero, services strip, footer, CTA bands) with off-white content sections (service descriptions, about text, forms) so gold and silver stay eye-catching rather than fatiguing on an all-dark site.

**Typography.** A serif display face (e.g. Playfair Display) for the hero headline and section intros echoes the engraved serif "JRM" lettering in the badge; a bold condensed sans (e.g. Oswald) for subheadings and buttons matches "TOP BUILD LTD." / "COMMERCIAL & RESIDENTIAL JOBS" in the badge; body copy uses a plain, highly legible sans (e.g. Inter or Work Sans) so long-form text on service pages stays easy to read.

**Icon system.** The five thin gold-outline circular icons already in the logo artwork (building/renovation, pergola frame, decking planks, diagonal floor planks, bath/shower) were extracted and should be reused as-is across the services strip, services hub, gallery filters, and service page headers, rather than replaced with generic stock icon packs — they're distinctive and already on-brand. Any additional icons the site needs (phone, email, pin, star, checkmark) should be custom-drawn in the same thin gold-line, circle-badge style so the set reads as one family.

## 6. Key feature specs

**Quote/enquiry form** — Fields: name, phone, email, property address/suburb, job type (dropdown pre-set to the five services + "Other"), commercial or residential toggle, preferred contact method, message/details, optional photo upload. Submits to the owner's email (junrobarios@gmail.com) with an autoresponder confirming receipt and expected response time.

**Photo gallery** — Category-filterable grid (All / Renovation & Alteration / Pergolas / Decking / Floor Planks / Bathroom Renovation) using the same five icons as filter buttons; lightbox view on click; captions with suburb + brief description double as long-tail SEO text.

**Testimonials & reviews** — Static curated quotes to start, with a live Google Business Profile review widget/link added once the business has a public listing, so trust content updates itself over time.

**Service area** — Simple suburb list grouped under "Based in Hornby, servicing Christchurch & Canterbury," with a short note that jobs slightly outside the list are still worth enquiring about — avoids the cost/complexity of a live interactive map while still answering the "do you cover my area" question.

## 7. SEO & local search notes

Register/claim a Google Business Profile under "JRM Top Build Ltd" with the Hornby address, phone, and the five service categories — this matters at least as much as the website itself for local "builder near me" searches. Use unique, suburb-aware title tags and meta descriptions per page (e.g. "Deck Builder Christchurch | Decking Installation — JRM Top Build"), add `LocalBusiness`/`GeneralContractor` structured data (schema.org) including the NZBN, address, phone, and service list, and keep the NZBN visible in the footer for credibility and B2B trust. Each of the five service pages should target its own primary keyword rather than competing with the others, and gallery captions and testimonial locations should mention real Christchurch suburbs to reinforce local relevance.

## 8. Technical recommendations

For a business this size, a lightweight, low-maintenance stack keeps ongoing cost down: either a static site (fast, cheap to host, easy to keep secure) with a simple form-handling service (e.g. Formspree/Netlify Forms) for the quote form, or a mainstream website builder (Squarespace/Webflow) if the owner wants to edit content without a developer. Either way the build should be mobile-first (most visitors will be on a phone), keep photos compressed for fast loading on-site 4G, use HTTPS, and include basic analytics (Google Analytics or a privacy-friendly alternative) plus Google Search Console from day one so search performance is visible.
